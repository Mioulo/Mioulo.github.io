Hello! Welcome to my homepage.你好！欢迎来到我的个人主页。

I am a master's student in Control Theory and Control Engineering at Tianjin University. My research focuses on learning-based planning and control, with particular interests in end-to-end motion planning, multimodal learning, and embodied intelligence.我目前就读于天津大学控制理论与控制工程专业硕士。我的研究聚焦于学习式规划与控制，尤其关注端到端运动规划、多模态学习与具身智能。

My previous work has mainly centered on end-to-end planning for agile quadrotors, including monocular and omnidirectional visual planning, with lightweight onboard deployment and real-world flight validation. More recently, I started exploring vision-language-action models for Embodied AI.我此前的工作主要围绕敏捷四旋翼的端到端规划展开，包括单目与全向视觉规划，并完成轻量化板端部署与实机飞行验证。近期，我开始探索面向具身智能的视觉-语言-动作模型。

🔬 Research interests: aerial robotics, end-to-end planning, vision-language-action models, multimodal learning, embodied intelligence, sim-to-real transfer, and efficient robotic inference.🔬 研究方向：空中机器人、端到端规划、视觉-语言-动作模型、多模态学习、具身智能、仿真到实机迁移与高效机器人推理。

Geometry-Aware Monocular Planning for Agile Flight面向敏捷飞行的几何感知单目规划

To address the inherent scale ambiguity and limited geometric cues of monocular vision, developed a geometry-aware end-to-end planner that combines consecutive monocular frames with camera pose and learns from a privileged depth expert through cross-modal supervision. Built a 3DGS-based sim-to-real pipeline and achieved 8 m/s in simulation and 6.5 m/s in real-world flight.针对单目视觉固有的尺度模糊与几何线索不足问题，研发结合连续单目图像与相机位姿的几何感知端到端规划器，并利用特权深度专家进行跨模态监督。搭建基于 3DGS 的 Sim-to-Real 流程，仿真与实机最高飞行速度分别达到 8 m/s 和 6.5 m/s。

Lightweight End-to-End Planning from a Single Depth Image基于单帧深度图的轻量化端到端规划

Developed a lightweight end-to-end planner that fuses a single depth image with UAV state to predict terminal states and generate polynomial trajectories online. Optimized and quantized the model with RKNN, achieving approximately 20 ms inference latency on RK3588; the complete onboard system was delivered and passed acceptance testing.研发融合单帧深度图与无人机状态的轻量化端到端规划器，预测终点状态并在线生成多项式轨迹。基于 RKNN 完成模型优化与量化，在 RK3588 上实现约 20 ms 推理延迟；整套机载系统完成交付并通过验收。

Multimodal Omnidirectional Planning for Agile Flight面向敏捷飞行的多模态全向规划

Developed an omnidirectional planner that fuses multi-view depth, UAV state, and target information, with planning-expert guidance for anticipatory obstacle avoidance. Built the quadrotor platform and validated the system at approximately 6 m/s in simulation and 3 m/s in real-world forest environments.研发融合多视角深度、无人机状态与目标信息的全向规划器，并利用规划专家引导实现前瞻式避障。完成四旋翼平台搭建，在仿真和真实森林环境中分别以约 6 m/s 和 3 m/s 完成验证。
